WUL Ultimate: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

WUL Ultimate is a game made by an independent developer (JustinSRao). This policy covers the web version, the Windows and Mac apps and the iPhone/iPad app.

The short version

  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No accounts or passwords.
  • The game stores two things on your device: a display name (your athlete's name) and a random player ID (for example p_3fa1…). Your game settings, athlete look and tournament progress are also kept on your device.
  • When you play online, your display name and random player ID are sent to the game server so other players in your room can see your name and so you can reconnect to your athlete if your connection drops.

Online play

Online rooms run on Cloudflare Workers. A room exists only while it is in use. It is deleted after 5 minutes without activity. The server keeps the room's player list (display names and random IDs), the match settings and, for co-op tournaments, the bracket, only for the life of the room. Your in-game inputs are used only to run the match. They are not stored.

Like any website or internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver network traffic. We don't log, store or use it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What we don't collect

No email address, real name, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers for advertising, health or financial data. Your random player ID is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. A display name can be anything. Please don't use your real full name.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser, to remove everything stored on your device. Server-side room data is deleted automatically.
