Swipe throwing: press, drag in the direction you want the pass to go (up = forward, sideways = break), release to throw. Longer swipe = more power. A swipe that bends gives an inside-out or outside-in release (shown live next to the trail): for a right-hander, a backhand swipe curving left-to-right is OI, right-to-left is IO; for a forehand it's the reverse. Left-handers are mirrored. Raise the camera (look higher) for a floaty, high release; look lower for a flat bullet. A short swipe is a quick dish, a long swipe is a huck. Pass assist nudges the throw toward the teammate you swiped at, but wind, rain and your thrower attributes still apply.
Phones & tablets: left thumb stick to run (push to the edge or double-tap it to sprint) · drag on the right side to look · with the disc, swipe on the right side to throw (BH/FH button switches, or put a second finger down for a forehand) · JUMP, DIVE, CALL and DISC CAM buttons · emote buttons appear after you score or assist.
Rules (automatic referee): catch in the attacking end zone to score. Drops, blocks, interceptions, out-of-bounds and stall-10 are turnovers; you can't run with the disc (pivot only). The referee is a video-game adaptation of self-officiated ultimate — contact on a receiver is called as a foul, picks are called and the defender recovers.
